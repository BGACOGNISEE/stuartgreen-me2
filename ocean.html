Ocean and blue economy advisory

Strategy, capital and governance advisory for institutions working on the ocean. I work with development banks, sovereign and philanthropic funds, national ministries and ocean-linked business at the point where ocean ambition has to become something a treasury, a board or a fisheries directorate can execute and keep executing after the political cycle turns.

Current mandates

Senior Advisor, Healthy Oceans Programme, Asian Development Bank. Regional ocean strategy, portfolio coherence and programme design across fisheries, marine protected areas, coastal adaptation and blue economy investment.

Chief Technical Advisor, COAST Facility, UK FCDO Blue Planet Fund. Technical leadership across grant-making and advisory workstreams in Vietnam, the Philippines, Indonesia and Mozambique. Implemented by DAI Global UK.

Member, OCEAN Expert Committee, Blue Planet Fund. Specialist advisory body for the Ocean Community Empowerment and Nature Grants Programme.

What I am commissioned to do

Ocean and coastal strategy. Blue finance and pipeline development. Fisheries governance, monitoring and traceability. Programme and portfolio design. Board and institutional advisory.

Engagements run through Blue-Green Advisors Ltd, the oceans-only advisory firm founded in 2017.
