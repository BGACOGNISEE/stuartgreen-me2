The Regenerate Leap Wins Bronze in the 2026 Living Now Evergreen Book Medal for Leadership

The Regenerate Leap has received the Bronze Medal in the Leadership category of the 2026 Living Now Evergreen Book Medals.

The Regenerate Leap: How Leaders Transform Crisis into Enduring Growth has received the Bronze Medal in the 2026 Living Now Evergreen Book Medal for Leadership.

The official 2026 Living Now Book Awards results list The Regenerate Leap by Stuart J. Green as the Bronze Medal recipient in category EB4, Living Now Evergreen Book Medal for Leadership.

The Living Now Evergreen Book Medals were established to recognise books published since 2000 that contribute to positive global change.

The recognition reflects the central argument of The Regenerate Leap: that leaders facing irreversible change need more than resilience. When the conditions surrounding an organisation have structurally changed, attempting to restore the previous model can itself become a source of fragility.

The book instead sets out a process of regeneration, moving through three phases, RAZE, ENRICH and GROW, to help leaders identify what must be retired, build capacity under pressure and redesign for the conditions that now exist.

The 2026 Living Now Book Awards results were published on 12 August 2026.
