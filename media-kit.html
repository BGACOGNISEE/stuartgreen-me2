Short biography

Stuart J. Green is an ocean strategist, institutional adviser and author of The Regenerate Leap. For thirty years he has worked at the intersection of marine science, ocean governance, investment and institutional strategy. He founded Blue-Green Advisors, a specialist ocean advisory firm, in 2017.

Extended biography

Stuart J. Green began his career as a marine scientist, working with coastal communities and fisheries systems in Southeast Asia. Over the following three decades, that work developed into a career spanning international development, ocean governance, institutional strategy and advisory roles.

His principal practice helps development banks, governments, funds and ocean industries turn ambition into decisions and programmes that can be implemented. In 2017 he founded Blue-Green Advisors to provide specialist advisory services to organisations working across the ocean economy. He also advises boards and chief executives on operating models that no longer fit the conditions they face.

He is the author of The Regenerate Leap, which brings together ideas from this professional experience with his understanding of irreversible change, and introduces the REGENERATE method: Raze, Enrich and Grow.

Interview topics

Most requested topic: AI and organisational design.

AI and Organisational Design

Why AI is exposing the structural flaws organisations have been ignoring, and why the organisations getting the most from AI redesigned how they work first.

Board and CEO Misalignment

Why boards and CEOs talk past each other on strategy during transformation, and how to diagnose whether the problem is leadership or architecture.

The Broken Operating Model

How to identify when an operating model has quietly stopped working, and what competent leaders miss when the architecture is the constraint.

When Efficiency Becomes the Vulnerability

Why the most optimised organisations are often the most fragile, and what nature knows about regeneration that most CFOs do not.

The Resilience Trap

Why "bouncing back" is the wrong goal when the world is not going back to what it was, and what regeneration requires instead, including the discipline of strategic decommissioning.

Compound Disruption and Polycrisis

Why traditional crisis management fails when disruptions are simultaneous, structural, and permanent.

Leadership vs. Design

How to tell whether your organisation has a leadership problem or a design problem, and why the distinction determines everything.
