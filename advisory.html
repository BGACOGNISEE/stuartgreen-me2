Board Advisory

Operating Model Advisory for Boards and Chief Executives

Not every performance problem is caused by poor execution.

Sometimes the organisation is operating exactly as its structure allows, but that structure no longer fits the conditions it faces.

I work with boards and chief executives to examine that mismatch, understand its consequences and evaluate the options.

Discuss an Operating Model Challenge →

Signs the Operating Model May No Longer Fit

  • Decisions repeatedly escalate without resolution.
  • Organisational effort increases while progress slows.
  • Restructuring has not resolved persistent performance problems.
  • Teams are measured against objectives that no longer reflect strategic priorities.
  • Technology investment exposes weaknesses in governance, workflow or accountability.
  • Boards and executives disagree about the underlying cause of persistent problems.

How I Approach the Work

Step 1

Diagnosis

Examine the relationship between strategic objectives, operating conditions, governance, decision rights and capability.

Identify the arrangements that may be constraining performance.

Step 2

Redesign

Develop practical options for changing the operating model.

Make trade-offs visible so that the board or executive team can make an informed decision.

Step 3

Execution Support

Support implementation and transition through the period when existing arrangements are changing and new capabilities need to become operational.

The Regenerate Framework

The Regenerate Framework provides a structured approach to examining operating-model fitness.

Its thirteen-question assessment offers an initial reading based on the respondent’s answers.

It is an initial self-report assessment, not an independently validated institutional measurement or a substitute for a detailed institutional review.

Where AI fits

AI tends to expose what was already weak in an operating model. Applied to a well-designed structure it can compound value; applied to a misfit it can accelerate the misfit. The practical sequence is to establish whether the structure can carry the capability before building anything.
