Ocean strategy, capital and governance

Institutional and operating model advisory

The ocean work also exposes the institutional conditions that make strategy hold or fail. For boards, chief executives, funds and membership bodies, the work diagnoses where an operating model stopped matching its conditions, designs the change that can be authorised, and supports the transition while the new structure takes hold.

How engagements run

Diagnosis. A structured assessment of the gap between the model and the conditions it now faces, producing a written account of the specific gaps, their cost and the sequence for addressing them.

Redesign. Target operating model work covering decision rights, structure, capability and governance, with trade-offs stated clearly enough for a board to authorise.

Execution support. Advisory through the period when the previous model is being dismantled and the new one is not yet carrying load.

The method is set out in The Regenerate Leap.

Engagement

Engagements run through Blue-Green Advisors Ltd, the oceans-only advisory firm founded in 2017, from short technical advisory through to multi-year facility roles. Procurement through development-finance and government frameworks is familiar ground.
